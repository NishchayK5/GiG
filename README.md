# G08: Fix it with GiG

GiG is a platform for contractors and businesses to easily find jobs, and for customers to quickly connect with people who can complete tasks for them.

GiG will provide a way for users to put up listings for tasks that they need help completing or professional work they cannot do themselves. Once customers put these requests up, contractors or businesses can make bids on tasks that they want to take on. These bids will be the price the business is willing to complete the task for. The customer is then able to choose which bid they want to take, and the deal is completed.

This ensures customers are getting competitive pricing for their tasks, and businesses are able to stay busy through odd jobs. Customers will also be able to leave public reviews on businesses and contractors, which will keep them more accountable and allow other customers to have a better idea of what they are actually purchasing.

The audience will contain two main groups of users. The first is the customer side, which encompasses all people who need something fixed, a task done, or general help with something they are unable to complete themselves. It will be beneficial for the customer user group due to the flexibility and bid system. Since businesses have to bid against each other to give the customer the lowest price, the customer will always receive the benefit of industry competitiveness.

The second group of users in the audience are those looking for side jobs, general contractors, or people involved in businesses. GiG is valuable for the business user group as they will have quick access to local side jobs, see what is most needed in the customer market, and possibly make lasting customer connections for future jobs. 

GiG will be an improvement over current 'marketplace' applications as it is specifically for finding jobs/task doers, whereas other applications may only have that as a side feature. This means GiG will be able to contain a much deeper and more fleshed out service for that specific need.

The system is envisioned to be an Android application, and users will be able to use touch controls on their phone to navigate and utilize each feature.


GiG aims to achieve the following measurable goals:

- Average user satisfaction is **7 out of 10 or higher** (10 being best), measured through a survey after users complete a given task.
- Posting a listing for a job or task as a customer user can be done in **under 2 minutes on average**.
- Finding a specific job or task and bidding on it as a business user can be done in **under 3 minutes on average**.
- It requires **fewer than 5 clicks** to navigate to any page in the app when starting from the login screen.
- The application has an **average response time of less than 2 seconds**.

If these goals are acheived, our project will be considered successful.
